2) Guidance for local-data deletion (detailed)
Subject: How to delete local data — [App name]

Hi [User Name],

We confirmed your request. Because [App name] stores images and related data on your device by default and does not upload them to our servers, we cannot remove those files from our side. Please follow the steps below to delete local data:

Option A — In-app (if available)
1. Open [App name] → Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete local data.
2. Confirm and restart the app.

Option B — Remove app and data (iOS)
1. Open iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage.
2. Find and tap “[App name]”.
3. Tap “Delete App” — this removes the app and its local data.
4. Reinstall from the App Store if you wish to continue using the app.

Notes:
- Offload App will keep local data; choose “Delete” to remove data.
- If you use iCloud or other backups, please remove any backup copies that include the app data.

If you prefer, reply with a screenshot of your Settings → iPhone Storage page and we will point out the exact controls.

Regards,  
Support Team
