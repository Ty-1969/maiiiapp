FAQ
- Why does the app need the camera?
  To scan sheet music images for on-device recognition. Images are not uploaded by default.
- Can I request deletion of recognition data?
  This app performs recognition on-device and does not upload scanned images by default. If your recognition data exists only on your device, we cannot delete it remotely. To delete local data:
  1. Use the app's settings to remove or reset stored items (if available).
  2. On iOS: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → find this app → Delete App (removes app and local data).
  For assistance, email support with app version, device model, and a device identifier; we can provide step-by-step guidance but cannot delete device-local data remotely.
