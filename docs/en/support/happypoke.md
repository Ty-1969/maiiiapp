FAQ
- Why is camera permission needed?
  To take prize photos; images are stored locally.
- Can I request local data deletion?
  Because this app stores images and related data only on your device and does not upload them to our servers, we cannot delete those files remotely. To delete data:
  1. In-app: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete local data (if available).
  2. iOS: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → find "Happy Poke" → Delete App (this removes app and its local data). Offload will keep data.
  If you need guidance, email support with app version, device model, and a device identifier; we can advise steps but cannot perform remote deletion.
