Privacy — Happy Poke (App addendum)
Bundle ID: com.happypoke.app

Permissions and differences
- Camera: used to take photos for prizes. Photos are stored locally on the device and not uploaded automatically.
- Photo Library: used to select prize images; access is local only.

Uploads and third parties
- This app does not upload user photos or browsing data to our servers and does not include third-party analytics by default.

Contact

Local data deletion
Because Happy Poke stores images and related data only on your device and does not upload them to our servers by default, we cannot delete those files remotely. To remove local data:
1. In-app: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete local data (if available).  
2. iOS: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → find "Happy Poke" → Delete App (this removes the app and its local data). Offload will keep data.
If you need step-by-step help, email support with your app version, device model, and a device identifier; we can advise but cannot perform remote deletion.
