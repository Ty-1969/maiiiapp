Privacy — MaiiiFinale (App addendum)
Bundle ID: com.maiii.MaiiiFinale

Permissions and differences
- Camera / Photo Library: used to scan and import sheet music images. Recognition is performed on-device.
- The app does not perform text OCR uploading or cloud recognition per project ADR.

Contact
- privacy: maiiiapp2026@gmail.com

Local data deletion
MaiiiFinale performs recognition on-device and does not upload scanned images by default. If your scanned images or recognition results exist only on your device, we cannot delete them remotely. To delete local data:
1. Use the app's settings to remove stored items or reset data (if available).  
2. iOS: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → find "MaiiiFinale" → Delete App (removes app and local data).
For assistance, email support with app version, device model, and a device identifier; we will provide step-by-step guidance but cannot remove device-local data remotely.
