Privacy Policy — Common

Overview
We value user privacy and process data on-device whenever possible. This common privacy policy applies to all Maiii apps; each app has an app-specific addendum that discloses permissions and any third-party services used.

Data collected
- Minimal data needed to provide the service: device identifiers, in-app settings, and crash/log reports if enabled.
- Some features require device permissions (camera, photo library); specific use is documented in each app's addendum.

Purpose of processing
- Provide, maintain, and improve app functionality; perform diagnostics and user support.
- Any remote upload or third-party processing will be explicitly disclosed in the app-specific addendum.

Third-party services
- By default, apps do not integrate third-party analytics or upload browsing content. If we add third-party SDKs (e.g., analytics or crash reporting), we will update the policy and list them on the app page.

User rights
- To request access, export, or deletion of your data, contact us at the address above. We will respond following applicable laws.

Changes
- We may update this policy; the last updated date above will reflect changes.
